Privacy Policy

Kayler's Scanner · Effective date: 9 October 2026

Kayler's Scanner scans QR codes and barcodes into lists on your phone and exports them to Excel. It is built so that your data stays on your device. The developer does not collect, receive or store any of your data.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your lists, the scanned codes, their code type and scan time, list names, the device catalog (code patterns and device names) and your settings.
  • All of it is stored only on your phone: in the app's own storage (iOS app) or in your browser's storage for this site (web version).
  • It stays there until you delete it in the app or remove the app (or clear the browser's website data for the web version).

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • The app does not send your lists, codes, catalog, settings or camera images anywhere.

Camera

The camera is used only while the scanner is open, to read codes on your phone. Camera images are processed on the device and are neither saved nor sent. You can turn camera access off at any time in the iOS Settings; you can still type codes by hand.

Files you export and the clipboard

An Excel file or a backup file is created only when you tap the button for it. The app hands it to the iOS share sheet (or your browser's download), and you choose where it goes, for example Files, Mail or another app. Once you send it somewhere, that place's own privacy terms apply. "Copy codes" puts the codes of the open list on the clipboard only when you tap it.

Diagnostics

About → Diagnostics shows technical camera details on your screen and can copy them to your clipboard. Nothing is sent automatically; you decide whether to share it.

The web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages (github.com). When your browser loads or updates the app files, GitHub receives the usual technical information of a web request, such as your IP address, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The developer does not receive this information. The iOS app contains its own files and does not load them from the internet.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date.
